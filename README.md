# My First Money Adventure: French Edition

A French translation and localisation of the children’s financial literacy e book, **My First Money Adventure**, created for children aged 5 to 8.

## Project Overview

This project adapts the original English edition of *My First Money Adventure* into natural, simple and age appropriate French while preserving the warmth, meaning, illustrations and learning goals of the original book.

The story introduces young children to basic financial concepts through the everyday experiences of Amara, Leo and their family.

## Learning Themes

The book helps children understand:

- Saving
- Spending wisely
- Sharing
- Earning
- Making responsible money choices
- Understanding that small decisions can have financial consequences

## Translation and Localisation Approach

The French edition was not translated word for word. The language was carefully adapted to sound natural and engaging for French speaking children.

The localisation process focused on:

- Clear and simple French
- Child friendly vocabulary
- Warm storytelling
- Preservation of character names and personalities
- Consistent page layout
- Accurate meaning
- Cultural sensitivity
- Readability for children aged 5 to 8
